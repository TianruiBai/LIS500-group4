Bias in the technology we use

We made this site to explain a few ideas about bias and how they connect to technology. The resource guide covers implicit bias, intersectionality, and the IAT, including what we think about using its results. You can also find a little about our group, Shuji Nakamura's blue LED, and Laura Gómez's work on fairer hiring.
