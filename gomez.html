Laura I. Gómez

A founder working to make tech hiring fairer.

Who she is

Gómez was born in León, Guanajuato, Mexico, and moved to California as a child. She studied at UC Berkeley and UC San Diego, then worked at YouTube and Twitter, where she helped bring Spanish to the platform. Her experience as a Latina immigrant also shaped her interest in diversity and inclusion.

Why she is a tech hero

Gómez founded Atipica, which combined AI with human review to help recruiters find talent and aimed to reduce bias. She is also a founding member of Project Include, a nonprofit that pushes tech companies toward inclusion. Berkeley's profile describes her approach as interpreting recruiting data with empathy.

Why I chose her

Our bias guide explains how data and testing choices can leave groups out. Hiring software is one place that happens. Gómez worked on that problem in a field where Latina founders are rare.
